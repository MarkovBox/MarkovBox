Meeting MET-004: Sprint 2 Review

Date: 19-11-2026 13:30-14:00
Form: online
Members: Oktawian Bieszke, Mikołaj Domiński, Marcin Ptasiński, Oliver Tyszko, Szymon Zaporowski

Goal

Discuss tasks and goals reached.

Question Discussed

  • What should we focus on in our documentation, such as decisions taken?
  • How thoroughly do we need to document everything, especially if we want to enter the dean’s competition?
  • Any advice on whether there’s anything in particular we should focus on implementing to make our project stand out?

Notes

Progress report

  • Focus on documenting as much as possible.

  • Our project schedule has been approved.

  • We will fill up our DTP/DPP with our GitHub docs later.

  • Our synthesizer is going to implement polyphony.

  • Markov chains: discussing polyphony or monophony due to the Daisy Seed's limited memory.

  • Perhaps we'll add built-in effects for the synthesizer, or maybe sampling to make it even more sophisticated.

  • Maybe add switching between synthesis types? Sound timbre settings are also possible to implement.

  • Make this project and the documentation easily accessible so that users can easily build and flash their own models trained on their MIDI files.

  • Meetings are confirmed to occur every other Thursday at 11:00.

  • Budget for our BOM has been approved.

Conclusions

Discussed the current state of the project, and for now the work is going on smoothly. We are going to continue the creation of the FM sound engine and implement our Markov's model as well as focus even more on comprehensive documentation.
